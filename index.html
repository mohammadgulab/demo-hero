Sequence 02
Systems that think 
before you do.

    

      
SYS 01
Vector flight
Boot and palm thrusters balance each other many times a second, so the pilot thinks about where to go, not how to stay up.

      
SYS 02
Layered shield
Hardened outer plates take the hit. A soft inner weave spreads what gets through, keeping the pilot steady inside.

      
SYS 03
Neural link
A light headband reads intent and moves the suit with you. No joysticks, no menus, just the next step.

      
SYS 04
Self repair
Small cracks are found, mapped and sealed while the suit is still moving. The pilot gets a quiet note, not an alarm.

    

  






  
Not stamped out in a factory. Forged in fire, tuned by hand, tested in storm, and only then trusted with a life.
